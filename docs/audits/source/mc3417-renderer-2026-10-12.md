# Fixed-rate MC3417 renderer candidate - 2026-10-12

## Behavior and acceptance boundary

`rtl/mc3417_stream.v` is an unbound, single-clock Q24 renderer. At the intended 192 kHz sample-enable cadence, each decoded endpoint starts a causal 13-tick ramp from the current output; 13 is the ceiling of 192000 / 15469.5695. Signed division truncates toward zero, and the last tick snaps to the exact target. A new endpoint interrupts/restarts the ramp from the current output. Endpoint capture has priority over sample enable and holds output on that edge. A clock-transition-only event may coincide with an advancing sample tick.

Either a clock transition or a valid decoded endpoint clears the idle counter. After exactly 6,000 sample ticks without activity the output remains active. Tick 6,001 declares idle and captures a zero target while holding output; the next 13 sample ticks reach exactly zero. Counter saturation prevents overflow. Cold reset clears all state. The renderer has no warm CPU/clock-reset input: a warm reset must preserve renderer state and be handled at the decoder/clock-history boundary. This is a stated digital policy inspired by MAME, not established physical MC3417 timeout behavior.

The [MAME stream reference](mc3417-stream-contract-2026-10-12.md) depends on callback block size. This renderer uses a deterministic causal ramp instead; it does not claim exact MAME scheduling/interpolated PCM equality. Its first advancing sample adds one increment; MAME's callback ramp emits the old endpoint first. Board low-pass stages, gain, final sample conversion/clipping and mono mix are not included. Inputs must be synchronous; production sample-enable generation, CDC, reset/pause and binding remain separate gates.

## Full-flow synthesis and timing

The initial generic constant-divider implementation fitted but failed setup by **-11.835 ns**. Physical reports identify `pcm_q24` to `increment` through the divider. The replacement computes exact unsigned division of the 32-bit magnitude using multiplication by `ceil(2^34/13) = 0x4ec4ec4f`, then restores sign. Endpoint difference magnitude is at most 2^32-1. The reciprocal excess is 3; its worst error is less than 3/52, below the 1/13 remainder gap, so quotient is exact. Non-default ramp lengths retain generic division and are not fitted acceptance claims.
