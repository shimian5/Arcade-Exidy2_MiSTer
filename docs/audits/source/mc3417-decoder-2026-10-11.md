Sources live under ignored `simulation/reference_sources/`.

MC3417 processes falling clock transitions with a three-bit coincidence history. Digit zero increases the integrator, digit one decreases it. It adds/subtracts the previous syllabic step, then leaks the integrator; the syllabic state is updated afterward. Charge/decay and leak coefficients use the fixed 16-kHz design in the emulator, even though Mouse Trap's board clock is about 15.48 kHz. Initial filters/history are zero. Warm device reset clears clock history only; it does not recreate or zero those filters. A speech-CPU reset is independent of decoder lifecycle.

## Reuse decision and implementation

Neither adjacent `wolf-unit` nor Robotron CVSD RTL establishes MC3417 parity. Wolf uses a rising edge, opposite digit sign and linear coincidence growth; its VHDL integer expressions for the documented decay coefficients evaluate differently from their comments, and reset is unused. Robotron uses the Harris integer model and separate half-edge operations. No adjacent algorithm implementation was copied.

The new unbound [mc3417.v](../../../rtl/mc3417.v) follows the MAME bit-event model in Q24 fixed point. It retains BSD-3-Clause notice and original MAME copyright holders. Quantized constants: leak 15,760,736; charge/decay 16,517,109; minimum step 697,932; maximum step 18,377,762; normalized sample gain 5,120,000. Intermediate products are wide, signed where required; output stays signed normalized Q24 rather than prematurely clipping to 16-bit PCM. There is no arbitrary integrator saturation in the pinned recurrence. Constructor-equivalent cold reset and clock-history-only warm reset are separate inputs. Falling edge is the default; the rising-edge parameter exists for a rejecting comparison experiment.

## Remaining acceptance boundary

This qualifies decoded bit-event state and next-sample arithmetic against the pinned emulator. It does not establish a physical MC3417 transfer curve, complete MAME stream output, board waveform or audible voices. MAME's stream includes slice-dependent interpolation and a stopped-clock silence timeout; neither is in this event-state candidate. The source itself flags its interpolation slope as questionable. Board filtering, route gain, PCM rendering/limiting and clock generation remain explicit integration units.
